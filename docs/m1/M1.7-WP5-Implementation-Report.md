## 4. Product behavior implemented

`PresenceCoordinator.observe` already derives absence from heartbeat age against `PRESENCE.STALE_AFTER_MS`; that rule is unchanged. Only the cadence values changed:

| Constant | Before | After | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `PRESENCE.HEARTBEAT_INTERVAL_MS` | 20 s | 3 s | Peers re-observe on the beat; the beat bounds detection latency |
| `PRESENCE.STALE_AFTER_MS` | 60 s | 6 s | Two missed beats; worst-case detection 6 s + 3 s = 9 s, inside the ≤ 10 s threshold |

No new surface, no new event, no new column, no architecture change. ADR-014 is untouched; nothing here controls or observes OTT playback.

## 5. Tests added or updated

`tests/certification/presence/m1-presence.spec.ts` was rewritten within its existing home:

- Provisions four participants (was three), per the WP5 acceptance criteria.
- `CERT-PRES-01` measures readiness convergence across all four peers.
- `CERT-PRES-02` now observes absence the way the product does — applying the shipped `PRESENCE.STALE_AFTER_MS` to the shipped `room_presence` row — instead of waiting for a column flip that no component performs. The elapsed time is recorded as a measurement and compared to the document threshold; a non-converging observation stays `unmeasured`.
